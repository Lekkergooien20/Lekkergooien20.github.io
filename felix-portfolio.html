Roblox

I started playing Roblox when I was 8, on my dad's old computer, then moved onto my own PC around 2020 and stuck with it all the way through to 2025. I started coding in Lua at 12, and spent years building on Roblox Studio.

Sadly, I ended up terminated by Roblox's AI moderation over what I believe was a mistake, and wasn't able to appeal my way out of it, so that chapter's closed for now. But over the years I racked up more than 10 million total visits across my games and over 500K Robux, some through DevEx and the rest spent on items, games, and donations. I met a lot of great people and made real friends through creating and playing.
